About BeProject Lab

A community for taking projects from A to B, with people who have already walked the road.

Why it exists

We all have a project we have been thinking about for a while: a business, a book, a change of direction. Having the idea is not the hard part. The hard part is taking the first step, ordering the road ahead and not doing it alone.

BeProject Lab exists for that: so that people with a project find people who know how to give it structure, direction and experience.

Who we are

A small team. The person who founded BeProject Lab has spent years leading projects, teams and clients between Europe and Latin America.

The blog articles tell real cases from that road: a supply chain that had to be rebuilt, a client relationship that was breaking apart and teams that learned to own the result.

How we work

  • We review every account by hand. No fake profiles.
  • Your contact details are shared only if both people agree.
  • We do not sell your data and there are no ads.
  • No algorithms: people who choose each other.
  • Today, joining and using the community is free. Later there will also be a premium version, paid and optional.

What we are not

We are not an agency and we take no part in the agreements. We put people in touch; whatever they agree afterwards is up to them.
