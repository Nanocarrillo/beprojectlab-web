Your project on one page

The minimum structure to stop improvising. It takes 30 minutes to fill in.

Download the template (PDF)

Free, no sign-up needed. One A4 sheet to print or fill in on screen.

  1. GoalWhat you want to achieve and by when. One sentence you can measure.

    “Open the studio with 20 students before March.”

  2. ScopeWhat is in and, above all, what is not. Saying no is also progress.

    “In: classes and an online shop. Out: workshops for companies, this year.”

  3. Milestones3 to 5 checkpoints with a date. They warn you that something is wrong before it is too late.

    “Space ready: 15 Dec. Website: 15 Jan. First class: 1 Mar.”

  4. RhythmA weekly 30-minute review: what got done, what comes next and what is blocked.

    “Every Monday at 9:00, with a coffee and this page.”

Stuck on one of the pieces? In the community, verified project managers help you build it.
