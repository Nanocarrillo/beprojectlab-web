Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is BeProject Lab (beprojectlab.com), based in Spain. For anything about privacy, write to us at info@beprojectlab.com.

What data we keep

  • Your account: your email and password. The password is stored encrypted and nobody can read it, us included.
  • Your profile: your name, your profile types (one or more: project owner, project manager, coach or mentor), the link to your LinkedIn and, if you add them, one line about you, a description, your country, links to Instagram, TikTok or your website and how you prefer to be contacted.
  • What you post: your posts, your replies, your “I'm interested” messages and the resources you share (title, description, link and section).
  • Your connections and notifications: who you have connected with, through which post, and the notices you see on the website (for example, that someone is interested in your post).
  • Technical data: the IP address and access logs our providers keep so the service works and stays secure.
  • Your messages: whatever you tell us if you email us.

What we use it for

  • Creating your account and letting you use the community. Legal basis: the contract you accept when you sign up, that is, the terms of use (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Putting members in touch when they both agree: sharing your full profile with the people you connect with and notifying you on the website. Legal basis: the contract.
  • Reviewing the resources you share before publishing them, so the page holds useful and safe content. Legal basis: the contract and our legitimate interest in looking after the quality of the community.
  • Checking that your account belongs to a real person, by reviewing your profile and your LinkedIn. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe and free of fake profiles (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). A person does this check, not an automated system.
  • Sending you the emails the service needs, such as confirming your account, resetting your password or telling you about important changes. Legal basis: the contract.
  • Answering your messages. Legal basis: your own request and our legitimate interest in replying.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Verified members see your name, profile types, posts, replies and published resources. Resources are published only after review and nobody without an account can see them.
  • The people you connect with also see one line about you, your description, country, links and how you prefer to be contacted. Connecting needs both people to agree: one says “I'm interested” on a post and the other presses “Connect”.
  • Your “I'm interested” message is seen only by the author of the post.
  • Nobody else in the community sees your email.
  • People without a verified account cannot see anything in the community.
  • Our providers, which handle the data only to provide their service to us:
    • Supabase, Inc.: database and account management. The data is stored on servers in the European Union (Ireland).
    • Hostinger: website hosting and sending email from info@beprojectlab.com.
    • Google: the website's fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you open a page.

Supabase and Google are US companies. When a provider accesses data from outside the European Economic Area, it does so with the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have your account. If you delete it, your account, profile, posts, replies, interests, connections, resources and notifications are removed right away; our provider's backups are replaced over the following days. Emails you send us are kept for as long as we need to deal with them.

Your rights

You can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, object to its use, restrict it, or receive it in a format you can take to another service. You can edit your profile and delete your account yourself from "My profile". For anything else, write to info@beprojectlab.com and we will reply within one month at the latest.

If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency at www.aepd.es.

Minimum age

The community is only for people aged 18 or over.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. When you sign in, your browser stores a session key so you stay signed in. The service cannot work without it, so we do not ask for permission, and it is removed when you sign out.

Security

The connection to the website is encrypted (https), passwords are stored encrypted, and the database itself limits what each person can see and change.

Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will update the date above. If the change is important, we will let you know by email.
