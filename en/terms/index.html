What BeProject Lab is

BeProject Lab (beprojectlab.com), based in Spain, is a community for taking projects from A to B. People with a project (project owners) say what they need, and project managers, coaches and mentors reply or say “I'm interested”. When both people agree to connect, they see each other’s full profiles and how to get in touch. The community is there to connect people: whatever you agree with each other afterwards is between you, and BeProject Lab is not a party to it.

Who can use it

  • People aged 18 or over.
  • One account per person, with your real name and details.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password safe and for what is done with your account.

Account verification

We review every account by hand before giving it access to the community. We may reject an account if we cannot confirm it belongs to a real person, and suspend or delete it if these terms are broken.

Community rules

  • Treat others with respect. No insults, harassment or discrimination.
  • Post real projects and real replies. No spam, mass advertising or misleading messages.
  • Do not post other people's personal data or third parties' confidential information.
  • Only offer the help you can actually give.
  • Only share resources that are yours or that you have the right to share.
  • Nothing illegal.

If you see something that breaks these rules, write to us at info@beprojectlab.com.

Your content

What you post stays yours. You allow us to show it to verified members for as long as it is published. Your “I'm interested” message is seen only by the author of the post, and your full profile only by the people you connect with. The resources you share are published after review and only verified members can see them; whatever the link points to stays yours. You can delete it whenever you want. We may remove content that breaks these terms.
